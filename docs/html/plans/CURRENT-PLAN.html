1. What we are building and why

VidGen Studio makes personalised campaign videos. You give it one template video and a sheet of names. It makes one video per row, where the spoken name (for example "Krishan") is replaced by the row's name, in the cloned voice, with the lips matching. Every video carries a small "AI" label, and the voter list is encrypted.

Two ways to run it:

  • Mac local (the main product). All the AI runs on your Mac's GPU. Nothing leaves the Mac. Slower: a 24 GB Mac

works but swaps heavily.

  • Cloud GPU (Modal, later). Faster, but the data goes to a cloud service.

The C5 privacy rule: in cloud mode only demo data (a neutral demo template and made-up names) is allowed. Real voter data never goes to the cloud. The installer and the app enforce this.
