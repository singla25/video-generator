What I could not do

  • npx jscpd was refused by the harness classifier, so I did not use it and wrote a simple exact-block scan instead. It only finds identical normalised lines, so near-duplicates (renamed variables) are missed.
  • vulture reports 60 percent confidence only. I ignored the FastAPI route handlers (they look unused because of decorators) and checked every other hit with grep over backend/, scripts/, main.py and tests/. Dynamic use (for example getattr, entry points, Alembic) can still hide a use, so treat the lists as candidates, not proof.
