Test tree: AI label style (DD-32, task LB-RED)

The AI label gets a style setting. bubble (new default) is a small rounded badge "AI" at the bottom-right, drawn on top of the video, adding no height. band is today's behaviour (bilingual band, at least 10 % of the height, top). Requirement C2 is intentionally relaxed for the bubble (Admin decision, DD-32).

Defaults chosen (recorded here, backend-dev implements exactly these names)

ItemChoice
SettingSettings.label_style: Literal["bubble", "band"] = "bubble", env VIDGEN_LABEL_STYLE; anything else rejected (no silent fallback), like ai_backend
Render configRenderConfig.label_style: str = "bubble"; an unknown value raises ValueError; render_config(settings) copies it
Bubble geometryDD-36: diameter 48/1080 of frame width, margin 12/1080 from the right and bottom edges (was 9 % / 3 % under DD-32); helpers bubble_diameter(frame_width) -> int and bubble_box(frame_width, frame_height) -> (left, top, right, bottom) in vidgen.infrastructure.ffmpeg.label
LookDD-36: see-through faint black tint (alpha 40), white ring about 4 % of the diameter, white "AI" with a soft 1 px shadow; was an opaque dark disc (DD-32)
RendererPillowBubbleRenderer(latin_font: Path, text: str = "AI") next to PillowLabelRenderer, same render_label(width, height, out) port. Contract: writes a transparent RGBA PNG of exactly width x height (the frame size) with the bubble drawn at bubble_box. So the encoder keeps overlaying at 0:0 and needs no change.
Factorybuild_label_renderer(style, devanagari_font, latin_font) in the same module: band gives PillowLabelRenderer, bubble gives PillowBubbleRenderer, unknown raises ValueError
PipelineRowRenderer: bubble calls labels.render_label(template.width, template.height, out) and never calls band_height; band unchanged (width, band_height(...)); label_band_share ignored for bubble
Local AIlocal_label_band_height(settings, frame_h) returns 0 for bubble, unchanged (at least 10 %) for band
Qualitya bubble video is not failed for a missing band (checker never looks for a band)

Existing tests that relied on the default being a band now say label_style="band" explicitly: tests/integration/worker/test_render_row.py (2 tests), tests/e2e/conftest.py, tests/unit/ai_local/test_label_height.py. Until the field exists these fail with a TypeError (RenderConfig) and nothing else changes.

DD-36 — see-through bubble look (task BB-RED)

Decision DD-36 (Admin chose design B): diameter 48/1080 of the width, margin 12/1080, faint black tint (alpha 40), white ring about 4 % of the diameter, white "AI" with a 1 px soft shadow. The band style and everything else stay as they were. Tests first; the developer implements exactly these numbers.
