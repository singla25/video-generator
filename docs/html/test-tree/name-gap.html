Test tree: name gap after the new name (DD-34, task NG-RED)

After the new name, everything later in the video starts gap_s seconds later. The output is gap_s longer than the template. Video: the last slot frame is held for gap_s. Audio: the region that replaces the slot is gap_s longer (silence padded) and the tail keeps its place, shifted later by gap_s. Defaults: Settings.name_gap_s 0.25 s (env VIDGEN_NAME_GAP_S, range 0 to 1.0), RenderConfig.name_gap_s 0.0, plan_timing(gap_s=0.0).

Hold thresholds (CEO, DD-34)

The 10-frame figure was wrong: at 25 fps a 0.25 s gap is about 6 frames. Frames are decoded to RGB; the difference d is the mean absolute pixel difference on the 0-255 scale.

  1. Motion floor: d(output frame 10, frame 11) >= 2.0 (measured 4.34; first limit was 5.0).
  2. Hold: every d(held frame at output frame 50, each of frames 50..54) <= 0.5 AND <= motion / 5

(measured largest 0.009).

  1. Gap-0 control: at least one of frames 50..54 has d > 5.0 (measured largest 17.46).

The codec makes the held frames not bit-identical (libx264 CRF 18 chroma noise), so the hold uses a tolerance instead of exact hashes.
