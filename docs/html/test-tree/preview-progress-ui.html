ItemRule
APIbatch.preview.stage: `"preparing" \"voice" \"lipsync" \"finishing" \null (optional). /system/status gives ai_mode: "fake" \"local"`; absent on older servers.
Progress listWhile preview.status is PENDING: <ol role="list" aria-label="Preview progress">, four items with exact texts "Preparing", "Speaking the name", "Matching the lips", "Finishing". Earlier steps data-state="done"; current has aria-current="step" and data-state="current"; later data-state="todo". Null or missing stage = "Preparing" current. Hidden for READY, FAILED, NONE, STALE, no preview.
Local noteai_mode local and PENDING: role="note" "First run after a restart is slow, a few minutes. The AI models are loading."
Slow warningPENDING for 30 s (fake, unchanged) or 180 s (local): "Preview is taking longer than expected." and a Retry button.
Fake noteai_mode fake: role="note" "Fake AI is on: the video will have a beep instead of the name." inside the Preview panel, any preview state. Not in local mode, not while the status call loads or fails.

Unknown stage values are not in the contract. The tests only require no crash, the four steps, and at most one current step.
